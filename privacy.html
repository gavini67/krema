Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information Krema Dessert Haus collects through this website and the Krema Rewards card, why we collect it, who helps us handle it, and what choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Promise, no fine-print tricks, bestie.

The short version

  • We only email you our news (new menu items, events, promos) if you tick the box on the K-list sign-up. It is unticked by default, and you can unsubscribe any time.
  • Krema Rewards keeps your name, mobile number and stamp history. If you secure your card, it also keeps your email and a PIN that we can never see.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use advertising cookies.
  • You can ask to see, fix, or delete your data any time. Email us at kremaphilippines@gmail.com.

Who we are

The personal information controller is Krema Dessert Haus, Unit 112, Banig Bldg., A. Luz Drive, Parqal Mall, Parañaque, Philippines. Questions about privacy go to kremaphilippines@gmail.com.

1. The K-list newsletter

The sign-up form is in the footer of our website.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The exact consent sentence you agreed to when you signed up.
  • The date and time you signed up.
  • The IP address the sign-up came from. We keep this as proof of consent and to block spam sign-ups.

Why we collect it

To email you news about new menu items, events and promos.

Our legal basis

Your consent. The consent box is unticked by default, so you only join if you choose to.

Your control

You can unsubscribe any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by emailing us at kremaphilippines@gmail.com. We do not sell or rent the list. The list is sent through Brevo, our email platform (see service providers below).

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe, we keep only a minimal record that you opted out, so we don't email you again.

2. Krema Rewards / Krema Club card

This covers the loyalty card on our Rewards page.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your stamp and reward history.
  • If you secure your card with an account: your email address and a 6-digit PIN. The PIN is stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider, so we never see it.

Why we collect it

To run the loyalty card, to let our staff find your card, and to let you recover your card if you lose access.

Emails from Rewards are not newsletters

Account emails, such as your sign-up code or a PIN reset, are transactional. They are not newsletter consent, and joining Rewards does not subscribe you to the K-list. Those are two separate things.

How long we keep it

For as long as your card is active. You can ask us to delete it any time at kremaphilippines@gmail.com.

The homepage shows embedded content from Google Maps, Instagram and TikTok. Those companies serve that content and are governed by their own privacy policies. They may set their own cookies when it loads.

4. Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising cookies. The Rewards page saves your member code and login session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser data removes it.

5. Security

Access to your data is limited to staff accounts. Our database tables are not directly readable by the public.

6. Your rights and how to contact us

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is collected and used;
  • access the data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent;
  • request erasure or blocking of your data;
  • data portability, meaning a copy of your data in a usable format; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
